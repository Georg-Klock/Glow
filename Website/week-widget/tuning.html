Ring halo tuning — desktop tier, 2x

The halo numbers were measured on letterforms. A ring is a much thinner shape, so each row below is the open ring cut at a different radius and strength (radius as a fraction of the cell, strength as a fraction of the peak). Judge on a screen with headroom in Chrome or Safari; on any other screen every plate tone-maps to the same grey and this page says so. The first row is what the card ships with. The live CSS ring at the far left of each row is the SDR fallback, for size comparison only.

This screen reports no extended range, so the plates below are tone-mapped and cannot be judged here.
