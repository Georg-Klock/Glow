Agents work all night minutes at a time, around the clock, no person in it

  1. An agent pulls in the issue backlog from GitHub. Claude plans with the higher models and codes with the lower ones; Codex builds cheaper for the long sessions.
  2. They test as they build, on simulators — dozens of runs, no hardware — and on the debug phone, tethered hardware for agentic testing.
  3. Tested builds go back as a pull request, one branch per change. The gates, the Xcode suite and CI, run on it, and main merges when green.

Then round again, without me.

The glow leaves the machines here, because no gate can judge it.

A human reacts twice a day, 45 minutes — the large loop, the one I am in

  1. Main ships the latest build to my phone each morning and evening. TestFlight on my iPhone is where I interact with it on real hardware, 45 minutes before work and again in the evening.
  2. Georg, the only human in the loop, reacts to what it is actually like.
  3. Claude interviews me with the lower models, then writes the issues and the prompts the agents run.

Back into the night's work.
